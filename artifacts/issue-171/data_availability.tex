\section*{Data availability statement}

The code, the exact software environment and a data archive with every
result reported in this article are archived on Zenodo
(\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24162839})~\cite{kgsemembed2026zenodo}
as release \texttt{v0.3.0-phase2-results} of
\url{https://github.com/swntsvt/kgsemembed}. The archive contains, for every
condition and dataset, the result files and per-source outcomes from which
all tables, figures and statistical tests are regenerated without the models
or the datasets, together with the stage-1 candidate lists and the stage-1
baseline. It holds entity identifiers, scores and metrics only; no text of
any dataset is redistributed.

The five datasets are available from their original releases, and the
input files used are byte-identical to them: the Bio-ML 2022
\texttt{snomed.body-fma.body} equivalence-matching task~\cite{he2022bioml},
the OAEI Anatomy track~\cite{bodenreider2005mice,oaei2024anatomy}, the OAEI
Conference track with reference alignment ra1~\cite{zamazal2017ontofarm,oaei2024conference},
the OAEI Knowledge Graph track (memoryalpha--stexpanded)~\cite{hertling2020kgtrack,oaei2023kg}
and the OpenEA v2.0 D-W-15K (V2) dataset~\cite{sun2020openea,openea2020dataset}.
The SNOMED CT ontology of the first dataset is subject to the SNOMED CT
licence and is available only to licence holders.
