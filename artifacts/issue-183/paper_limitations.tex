\paragraph{Properties of the verbalised text.}
Two properties of the input text are kept by design rather than cleaned
per dataset. First, OpenEA removed the labels of D5's entities, so an object
without a label is written as its opaque identifier (a DBpedia
\texttt{E\ldots} or Wikidata \texttt{Q\ldots} number). The two graphs share
none of their roughly 8{,}000 object identifiers each, so these tokens carry
no signal across the graphs; they make up 9--63\% of the tokens of the
key--value, template and walk conditions on D5, depending on condition and
side, and all of a source's label (V1). Removing them would require a
dataset-specific rule after results were known, so we report D5 with them
and treat its results accordingly. Second, V3, V4 and V6 write every
predicate of an entity, including those we exclude from V8 as annotation or
page metadata; on D4$_{\mathrm{in}}$ these supply 34--52\% of the tokens,
most of it the wiki page's abstract and link text. Literal values on D5 are
written without OpenEA's inlined datatype, as in stage~1, which also drops
the currency unit of 6{,}554 monetary values.
